Checks

Every set in the data is verified against what it is supposed to represent: where it lands, how high it goes, who hits it, and how it relates to the sets around it. Edit the SETS array and this page re-grades it.

Hang time and apex come from a gravity model: the ball leaves the setter's hands just above the tape and is contacted above the net at the landing spot. Tempo is read from where the hitter is when the setter touches the ball: minus, already leaving the floor; first, planting; second, mid-approach; third, waiting for the set. A hitter starting outside the sideline takes a full 1.0 s approach; one starting inside the court (a middle, or the opposite on a Back 2) 0.75 s; a back-row run 1.2 s. Hang times are checked against measured Olympic set flight times, and the peak heights follow from them and from the hitters' reach (the Club / College / Pro control in the 3D view; checks are graded at club reach).
